# Source material

Original documents from the session, kept unchanged. The SHA-256 for each file is in `CHECKSUMS.txt`.

Both PDFs were generated with AI and exported from Google Docs. They were emailed by Rob Tyrie to session participants on October 5, 2024, and recovered from that email on October 18, 2026.

| File | Original title | Pages |
|---|---|---|
| `patent-application-draft.pdf` | Blockchain-Enabled Adaptive Educational Game Factory Platform with Integrated Economy and Ethical AI | 5 |
| `platform-description-v3.pdf` | Adaptive Dynamics AI-Powered Gaming: A No-Code Platform for Game Curators and Content Creators (v3) | 12 |

`patent-application-draft.pdf` is written in the form of a patent application, but it is not a patent. It is published here as prior art.

`platform-description-v3.pdf` restates the same concept in a longer, structured form.

## A note on names

The two documents list participants differently:

- `patent-application-draft.pdf` lists five people as "Inventors and Owners": Rob Tyrie, Valentin Bercovici, Andrew Robinson, David Shepherd and Geoff Frodsham.
- `platform-description-v3.pdf` lists eleven creators.

The documents are kept as they were written. The whole work is shared under CC BY 4.0, and [CONTRIBUTORS.md](../CONTRIBUTORS.md) is the reference for credit. It draws on both documents and was confirmed by the archive's host on October 18, 2026. Corrections are welcome by issue or pull request.
