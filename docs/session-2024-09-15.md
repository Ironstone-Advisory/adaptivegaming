# The session: September 15, 2024

## Setup

A video call of eleven people: computer scientists, engineers, a rocket scientist, and business people. One person drove the AI tools on a shared screen; everyone else talked. The session was recorded.

## The method

The point of the day was the method, not the game.

| Step | Input | Output | Time |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | A prompt for a simple card game | A working React component | Minutes |
| 2 | The component | The insight: one transformation layer yields a thousand themed variants | Minutes |
| 3 | The component | Requirements, design doc, config: enough to regenerate the game from documents | Under an hour |
| 4 | The documents | A press release for a notional company | Minutes |
| 5 | The press release and the group | A platform architecture diagram, built up around the table | An hour or so |
| 6 | Everything above | A patent-style write-up of the full platform | Under an hour |

The pattern is **software first, then documentation derived from software, then the wrapper**. Documentation detailed enough for a model to work from becomes the engine for variants. The participants called the effect "expansion through synthesis".

## What the platform concept covered

- A no-code factory where curators and players create casual games.
- Headless, API-first games that can be skinned for any brand or event.
- Multiplayer play in a room: point a phone at a QR code on a screen and join.
- An in-platform economy, educational uses, and ethical AI by design.

## Afterwards

On October 5, 2024 the write-up and a summary were circulated by email for feedback. The concept was not pursued as a company. It is preserved here as a dated public record.
